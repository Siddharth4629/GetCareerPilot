Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about GetCareerPilot? Here are answers to the most common queries.

Is GetCareerPilot only for engineering and computer science students?
No! GetCareerPilot supports 12 broad sectors including Medicine, Healthcare, Law, Public Policy, Chartered Accountancy, Creative Arts, Aviation, Teaching, and Vocational Trades. We believe every student deserves personalized guidance regardless of stream.
How does the Class 10 stream selection guide work?
Our Stream Selection Guide compares Science PCM, Science PCB, Commerce, Arts/Humanities, and Vocational courses. It evaluates required subjects, typical workloads, future undergraduate courses, and top professions to help you make an informed decision.
What is the RIASEC assessment and how accurate is it?
The RIASEC framework is a globally recognized psychological model developed by Dr. John Holland that groups career interests into six archetypes: Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising, and Conventional. Our 10 calibrated questions compute your top personality traits and suggest fitting careers.
How does the AI Mock Interview Cockpit score responses?
The Cockpit evaluates your answer across 4 key criteria: Relevance & Accuracy, Technical / Domain Depth, Clarity & Structure, and Delivery. It provides a numeric score from 0-100, highlights your key strengths, identifies specific gaps, and provides an exemplary model answer.
Is GetCareerPilot free to use?
Yes! All core tools including the RIASEC Assessment, Stream Selection Guide, College & Exam Explorer, Resume ATS Analyzer, AI Mock Interview Cockpit, and Daily Planner are 100% free for students and job seekers.
